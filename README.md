# Meditrack-medication-reminder-app-Project
Chosen SDG: Good Health and Well-Being (SDG 3)

Team members:
Edric Sebastian Agustandra – Project Manager, Scrum Master
Aishath Rany Riyal - UI/UX designer. Business Analyst, Project Management Assistant

Project details:
An app for patients to log medications, set reminders, and share reports with their doctor.
Primarily targeted towards patients with chronic conditions who have to take long term medication.
Elderly patients who may have to manage multiple medications and are prone to forgetting and/or mixing up medications.

SDLC cycle summary:
We chose SDG 3 and brainstormed a couple of ideas before settling on a medication reminder app. From here, we decided to do some market research on similiar existing apps and we listed their core functionalities, unique selling points and cons. We did further research for requirement gathering by sending a survey through google forms and we analysed the responses. From here we finalized the requirements, scope and constraints of the project. We designed the use case diagram and class diagram based on the requirements gathered using draw.io, then moved on to the actual wireframing on figma. We made assets for the prototype through canva then created the first test prototype which you can find attached in the design folder. After gathering feedback we created some more screens and created our final prototype. We had some users go through the prototype (through teams/zoom) to see if it met their expectations of what a medication reminder app should look like and gathered feedback on the functionality. The overall feedback was positive, we got many suggestions for other features that could be included in the app.
